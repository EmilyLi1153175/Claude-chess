Opening Files with Lucas

Workbook page 101 · Chapter 21b, opening a file (开线). Tap a piece, then tap where it goes. If you need help on a puzzle, you get a practice puzzle with the same idea.

What is "opening a file"? A file is a column on the board (the a-file, the b-file…). Rooks and queens love open files. Sometimes you give up a piece to knock a pawn out of the way, so your rook or queen can rush down the file at the enemy king or queen.
